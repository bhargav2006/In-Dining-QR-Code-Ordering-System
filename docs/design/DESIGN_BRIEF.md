# Design Brief — Restaurant QR Ordering System

## Creative Direction

Design a premium, contemporary restaurant technology product.

The design should combine hospitality-inspired visual character with the precision and usability of a modern software product.

Avoid generic AI-generated dashboard aesthetics.

## Customer Experience

The customer experience should feel like interacting with a thoughtfully designed restaurant ordering product.

Priorities:

- Mobile-first layout.
- Menu items presented with strong visual hierarchy.
- High-quality food photography when genuine assets are available.
- Easy category browsing.
- Clear item descriptions and pricing.
- Convenient quantity selection.
- Visually clear cart and order summary.
- Obvious ordering actions.
- Fast and understandable checkout.
- Clear order status feedback.

Avoid unnecessary navigation steps.

## Staff and Kitchen Experience

Priorities:

- Fast access to incoming orders.
- Clear order status.
- Easy-to-scan information.
- Visible order details.
- Efficient status updates.
- Minimal interaction friction.

## Administrator Experience

Priorities:

- Clear information hierarchy.
- Consistent navigation.
- Practical management interfaces.
- Well-organised tables and forms.
- Meaningful summaries.
- Consistent interaction patterns.

## Visual Identity

Use the approved Global Design System:

- Verdant Signal Light.
- Obsidian & Forest Dark.

Do not invent another brand palette.

## Visual Design Rules

Prefer intentional differences between:

- Navigation.
- Customer menu.
- Product detail.
- Cart and checkout.
- Order management.
- Administrative pages.

Do not use the same card-based layout for every screen.

Do not introduce decoration without a user-experience purpose.

Use typography, spacing, imagery and composition to create individuality.

## Motion

Use subtle transitions that support usability.

Avoid excessive animations and unnecessary background effects.

## Responsive Priorities

Customer ordering: mobile first.

Staff operations: responsive and information-efficient.

Administration: desktop-first with functional tablet and mobile adaptations.
